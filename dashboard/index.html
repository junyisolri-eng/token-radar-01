    How the value is worked out. Implied value = TVL × the median market-cap/TVL of live-token protocols in the same category (taken from this snapshot, never from a name) × a growth multiplier from the 7-day TVL change (capped at +100%) × a chain multiplier (more chains, wider reach) × a maturity multiplier (surviving longer counts more) × an already-trading correction (1, except USDD and Bittensor dTAO, whose tokens were verified to already trade — their implied value is a small residual and they sit at the bottom of the board). A protocol is listed as pre-token when its record carries no token — that is what the snapshot records, not a promise it has none.
